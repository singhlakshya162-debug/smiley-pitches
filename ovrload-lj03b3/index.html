The gap

Every ad is a sale

  • All 30 of your most-shown ads lead with a discount or a deadline (“49% off for a limited time only”, “sale ends tomorrow”, “FLASH SALE £15 A BAG”). After Black Friday there's nothing in the account that works without a sale.
  • Among those 30, your 84-day line never gets a clean static. It runs on creator video and price cards, so the product, the dose and the Informed Sport mark rarely get the frame.
  • A friendly heads-up: “sale ends tomorrow” on 80-day-old ads is the kind of urgency the CMA and ASA look at, and so is naming Mounjaro, Ozempic and Wegovy on your GLP-1 pages.
