The gap

Your science is a caption, never a picture

  • Nearly every one of your 30 most-shown ads says “founded by female neuroscientists & nutrition experts”, but neither Alice nor Dr Mitra appears in any of the creatives.
  • Almost every ad leads with a discount or free gifts (£38 → £19, £43 → £15, gifts worth £135+). A trust-led ad gives the account something that doesn't depend on the price.
  • A friendly heads-up: in the UK, food supplements can't claim to relieve PMS, period pain or PMDD, whatever a trial shows, so lines like “88% had significantly reduced symptoms”, “relief from a luteal crisis”, the ADHD-rage framing and a customer calling the capsules “life saving” are the kind the ASA and Meta look at closely. The ads below use the authorised vitamin B6, C, iron and magnesium wording instead.
