The gap

Christmas is the moment, and the calendar isn't in the ads

  • Your 12 Days of Wellbeing calendar went on sale on 9 November (£99, made in limited numbers), but none of your 30 most-shown ads mention it, gifting, or the Black Friday offer on your site.
  • Two products, two messages. Every one of those 30 ads is CreaGlow or the Vegan Protein Blend, so someone looking for a gift in December never sees one.
  • Your best ideas sit in busy formats. The 80-day winner is a hand-written whiteboard, and the protein line runs on video stills with caption stickers. Clean statics of the same lines give Meta more to test.
  • A friendly heads-up: in the UK, collagen has no authorised health claims, so lines like “firmer skin” and “stronger nails & shinier hair” on the whiteboard, or “mental clarity” for creatine, are the kind the ASA and Meta look at closely. The ads below use the authorised vitamin C, biotin and creatine wording instead.
