The gap

The story lives in the copy, not the creative

  • No static tells the 8-month story. Its image versions show a “60 days risk free” offer render or a “One Gummy Six Mushrooms” diagram, so anyone who doesn't read the caption never sees it.
  • One clean static is a cheap test against those: the story as the headline, the three numbers, and your pack.
  • A friendly heads-up: mushroom benefits (focus, energy, immunity, “lower cholesterol”) aren't authorised health claims in the UK, and lines like “Only 64 left” or “three times what you'd usually see” need to be literally true and substantiated. The ad below sticks to counts, dose, serving, guarantee and where it's made.
